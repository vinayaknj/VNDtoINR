💡 Etiquette & Tips
  • Tipping: Not expected or mandatory. A small tip (5-10%) in upscale places or for exceptional service is appreciated but never required.
  • Handing Money: Try to use both hands when giving or receiving money to show respect.
  • Temples: Cover your shoulders and knees when visiting pagodas. Remove shoes if required.
  • Crossing the street: Walk slowly and predictably. Don't step back. Motorbikes will swerve around you.
  • Bargaining: Expected in markets (like Ben Thanh). Start at 40-50% of the asking price and work up with a smile.
🍜 Must-Try Food (by priority)
  • Hanoi: Phở Bắc (Northern Noodle Soup), Bún Chả (Grilled Pork & Noodles), Cà Phê Trứng (Egg Coffee), Chả Cá Lã Vọng (Turmeric Fish with Dill), Bánh Cuốn (Steamed Rice Rolls).
  • Sapa: Lẩu Cá Hồi (Salmon Hotpot), Lợn Cắp Nách (Roasted Free-Range Pork), Cơm Lam (Bamboo Sticky Rice), Gà Đen (Black Chicken).
  • Ninh Binh: Dê Nướng (Grilled Mountain Goat), Cơm Cháy (Crispy Rice Crust), Miến Lươn (Eel Glass Noodles), Ốc Núi (Mountain Snails).
  • Halong Bay: Chả Mực (Hand-Pounded Squid Cakes), Bún Bề Bề (Mantis Shrimp Noodles), Xôi Chả Mực (Sticky Rice with Squid), Hàu Nướng (Grilled Oysters).
🚫 Common Scams to Avoid
  • The Shoe-Shine Scam: Someone may point at your shoes or grab them to start cleaning/gluing them without permission, then demand a huge fee. Action: Firmly say "Không" (No) and keep walking. Don't let them touch your shoes.
  • Fake Taxis: Unmetered taxis that look like official ones but have rigged meters. Action: Use the Grab app, or only use official Mai Linh (green) or Vinasun (white) taxis. Check for uniforms and dashboard IDs.
  • The Photo Trap: Vendors quickly place their fruit baskets/pole on your shoulder for a "free photo," then slice a coconut and demand an exorbitant price. Action: Refuse the pole politely but firmly.
  • Cyclo Price Bait: Drivers agree on a low price but later claim it was "per person" or "per hour". Action: Write down the total agreed price before getting in, and clarify it's for the entire trip.
  • Bill Swapping: The 100k & 10k bills look similar (green). The 500k & 20k bills look similar (blue). Vendors may swap them when giving change. Action: Always double-check the zeros before handing over or accepting cash.
